Privacy Policy

gmmars1.me — Grandmaster Mars / GmMars Solutions. Last updated November 4, 2026.

The short version

This is a static website. It has no accounts, no sign-ups, no forms, and no comment sections. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. The site itself does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

What this site does not do

  • No user accounts or logins.
  • No cookies set by this site.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts.
  • No forms that submit information anywhere.
  • No sale or sharing of visitor data — there is no visitor data to share.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. As with any web host, GitHub's servers may record standard technical request information (such as IP addresses) to operate and secure the service, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. The site operator does not receive visitor analytics from this hosting.

Interactive pages

The interactive guide on this site runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you click or view there is transmitted to the site operator or anyone else.

Bookmarklets

The bookmarklets page offers optional tools you can drag to your bookmarks bar. A bookmarklet runs locally in your browser, only when you click it, on the website you are viewing at that moment. Some bookmarklets request data from the service you are viewing (for example, a public profile API) at your direction. Bookmarklets never send data to gmmars1.me.

External links

If a page here links to an external website, that site's own privacy policy applies once you leave gmmars1.me.

Changes to this policy

If this site ever changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, adding accounts or analytics — this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
